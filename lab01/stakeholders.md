# Stakeholder priorities

The Head of the IT department (S01) and the supervising lecturer/project manager (S02) need the PM's earliest attention because both have high influence and high interest. The sponsor controls investment, scope decisions and continuation gates, while the PM coordinates the work, maintains baselines and makes progress visible. They should agree on the charter, MVP acceptance criteria, cash cap and review evidence in week 1, then review progress at week 7 and pilot readiness at week 13.

**Veto stakeholder:** The data protection officer or registrar (S09) could stop the pilot if student-data handling, access controls or retention arrangements are unacceptable. Their review should happen before pilot readiness, not on launch day.

The CSV includes 11 stakeholders across sponsor, PM, team, user, operations, governance and external categories. Influence and interest ratings are initial planning estimates and should be revisited during later stakeholder-engagement work.
